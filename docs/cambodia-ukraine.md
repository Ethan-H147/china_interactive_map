# Cambodia and Ukraine

Added locally on 19 October 2026. No deployment is part of this addition.

Cambodia has 24 provinces and Phnom Penh capital. Names and PCodes are checked against the [NCDD gazetteer](https://db.ncdd.gov.kh/gazetteer/view/index.castle), including Khmer labels. Province geometry is the [OCHA COD-AB reference](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-khm), created in 2018 and reviewed in October 2024. The older 197-district polygons are omitted because the gazetteer lists 210 current district-level units. Province populations are [NIS/UNFPA 2024 projections](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ps-khm), not a census.

Ukraine has 24 oblasts, the Autonomous Republic of Crimea, Kyiv and Sevastopol. The [September 2025 OCHA reference](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-ukr), reviewed in December 2025, also supplies 126 mainland raions. Crimea child geometry is omitted because the source warns that its administrative levels do not nest consistently. Sevastopol’s full administrative territory uses the 2017 OpenStreetMap/geoBoundaries reference (ODbL 1.0), clipped to the same Crimean land silhouette so territorial sea is excluded. This corrects the COD-AB urban footprint. Kyiv and Sevastopol are not duplicated as raions, and the Chornobyl exclusion zone is not treated as a raion. English and Ukrainian names and PCodes are retained.

Crimea and Sevastopol belong to the Ukraine page following internationally recognised boundaries. Both have a visible [UN-sourced occupation note](https://ukraine.ohchr.org/en/reports/crimea-and-occupied-territory-secretary-general). The atlas does not draw a live front line. Previously, the background source included Crimea inside its Russia feature, which was suppressed when the Russia atlas outline loaded; that atlas outline itself excluded Crimea. Adding Ukraine's complete silhouette closes this coverage hole from every page.

Ukraine's regional populations are historical estimates at 1 January 2022 from the State Statistics Service. The exact regional table is parsed and its 25 available regional totals must sum to 41,167,335. The publication has no figures for Crimea or Sevastopol, so none are inferred. The public July 2026 humanitarian population metadata explicitly restricts its intended use, and its restricted estimates are not incorporated. National population and current-price GDP come separately from World Bank WDI and can use different coverage and estimation methods.

Water masks use the retained Natural Earth reference. The obsolete Kakhovka Reservoir water polygon is excluded after its [documented drainage](https://ukraine.un.org/en/270890-kakhovka-dam-destruction-one-year); river and floodplain detail still has source-scale limitations. Tonle Sap is excluded from Cambodian land fills. Shared borders use complete arcs from existing atlas neighbours rather than independently simplified strokes.

Raw downloads and SHA-256 hashes are retained in scripts/boundary-sources/cambodia-ukraine/. Build with node scripts/fetch-cambodia-ukraine.mjs and node --max-old-space-size=10000 scripts/build-cambodia-ukraine.mjs, followed by the world-background, flag-image, region-colour and compact-data builds. npm start serves the local preview at port 4174.
