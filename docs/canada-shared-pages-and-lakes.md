# Canadian shared city pages and major lakes

The 2021 Statistics Canada classification contains 12 census divisions made up of exactly one municipal census subdivision. Their retained land areas, geographic extents and July 2025 population estimates match. Both original identifiers open one page, with one search result, one statistics panel and the municipal flag. The two map levels retain their own geometry IDs and saved-view levels. Back navigation skips the duplicate identity; the city is not listed as its own child.

The shared pages are Lévis, Shawinigan, Sherbrooke, Laval, Mirabel, Gatineau, Rouyn-Noranda, Ottawa, Prince Edward County, Kawartha Lakes, Toronto, Hamilton. The retained [official classification](../scripts/boundary-sources/canada-shared-pages/sgc-2021.csv.gz) and [page registry](../dist/data/europe/canada/shared-place-pages.json) record the exact pairs.

Montréal remains separate because its census division contains 16 municipalities. Greater Sudbury also remains separate: its 2021-boundary July 2025 city estimate is 188,504, while the census division estimate is 188,645, including 141 residents in Wahnapitei 11. The population cards explicitly distinguish those scopes, and the reserve retains its own page. Chatham-Kent and its census division likewise stay separate because the latter includes Moravian 47. [Statistics Canada classification for Greater Sudbury](https://www23.statcan.gc.ca/imdb/p3VD.pl?CLV=3&CPV=3553&CST=01012021&CVD=1346800&Function=getVD&MLV=4&TVD=1346772).

Great Bear Lake, Great Slave Lake, Lake Winnipeg and Lake Athabasca are removed from administrative land fills at every level, telephone area-code overlays, country previews, the flight background, and the resident globe overview. Overlapping telephone footprints are clipped independently. Administrative boundary strokes stop at the lakes; selected land outlines follow the resulting shore. All 5,161 census subdivisions remain present. Published populations and land areas are unchanged by the water masks.

The source is [Natural Earth v5.1.2, 1:10 million lakes](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-physical-vectors/10m-lakes/), public domain. The four named source geometries, including their island holes, are retained with source hashes. This is generalized display hydrography. The globe overview’s coarse Canadian geometry is normalized before clipping to preserve its mainland. Other countries’ background features remain unchanged.

Rebuild shared identities with `npm run build:canada-shared-pages`, and water displays with `npm run build:canada-lakes`. Canadian local, municipal-facts, telephone, world-land and globe-overview build paths retain the relevant changes. The lake pass records its source signature and is byte-stable when repeated.

Validation covers all 12 identities and both old links, search, flags, saved views, parent navigation, Sudbury and Montréal exclusions, all four lakes at every administrative level and telephone view on desktop and phone, and globe fallback after changing countries. The geometric screen uses 1,588 lake-interior probes and more than 184,000 fill checks, checks surrounding land and background mainland, and confirms Alberta/Saskatchewan’s land border against a retained pre-mask reference outside Lake Athabasca. Existing U.S. shared pages, Canadian local geography, telephone coverage, municipal facts, and major-city flags are also checked.

The world-land validator has a pre-existing size-budget failure: the unchanged HEAD source is 21,300,166 bytes against a 5,000,000-byte retained-source limit. This task adds about 32 KB to that source. Deployment checks verify the compact copies independently. The globe overview test’s Sahara probe now checks its existing Algeria routing; the prior data already routes that location to Algeria rather than the unassigned background.
