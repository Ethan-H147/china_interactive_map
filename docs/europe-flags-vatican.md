Vatican City is a sovereign country with `parent: 'italy'` for navigation only. Open Italy → Vatican City, or `#vatican-city`. It uses the shared regional renderer, country loader, map scene, flag viewer and saved-view format. Subpages stay out of the top-level country menu, whose current item remains Italy. Their addition dates remain separate in country-history.json.

The retained OSM enclave geometry excludes the Holy See’s extraterritorial properties in Italy. Area comes from the Vatican State geography page (0.44 km²); no population or GDP is inferred. Landmark labels are reference points, not subdivisions. The square national flag follows the 2023 Fundamental Law artwork. Vatican colors and the original decorative basilica drawing are scoped to this page.

Regional flags: scripts/europe-expansion-flag-files.json maps exact administrative IDs to source files. Germany includes all 16 states and 104 independent cities in the 2024 catalogue. Dutch coverage is all 12 provinces. Belgium includes three regions and all ten provincial designs. Hainaut and Liège are documented as unofficial provincial banners of arms, explicitly captioned as such; no later formal adoption was verified. The Brussels regional and municipal flags are different. German municipality records inherit their own independent-city flag by parent ID. No image is chosen from a bare city name at runtime.

Original artwork, thumbnails, dimensions, Commons credit, license and checksums are retained locally in data/europe-expansion-flag-sources.json and the shared flag viewer catalogue. Rebuild with npm run fetch:europe-expansion-flags; unchanged flags reuse pinned originals. --refresh explicitly updates artwork from Commons.

Vatican and Italy share one retained enclave ring at every level. scripts/reconcile-vatican.mjs changes seven enclave paths in six audited Italy files and backs up originals under artifacts/vatican-enclave-backup. Italy owns the international stroke; the Vatican selection uses the identical polygon. Run npm run build:vatican after rebuilding Italy independently.

Municipal flag audit (17 October 2026): all 325 municipalities with at least 50,000 residents in the atlas 2024 population snapshot, plus provincial capitals: Germany 197, Netherlands 95, Belgium 33. The retained scripts/europe-city-flag-audit.json links every selected administrative ID to its checked source; tests derive the population threshold independently and reject coverage omissions. All earlier German independent-city flags remain. Municipal and provincial designs are distinct (Amsterdam, Groningen, Antwerp and Liège included). Two unavailable reusable flag images, Garbsen and Langenhagen, use explicitly captioned renditions of municipal colors and retained public-domain arms, with source artwork and checksums under scripts/flag-renderings/. Gooise Meren is captioned as an unofficial municipal logo flag; Midden-Groningen uses its municipal logo flag as specified by Article 2 of its 2024 flag protocol. Bruges uses the retained Vlag van Brugge.png reference artwork rather than the SVG described as an unofficial variant.

Status references: https://www.crwflags.com/fotw/flags/be-wlg.html and https://www.crwflags.com/fotw/flags/be-wht.html report the provincial decisions to retain their unofficial banners of arms; Liège provincial communications confirm the traditional arms still appear in official protocol. https://lokaleregelgeving.overheid.nl/CVDR717425/1 documents Midden-Groningen’s logo flag.

Unofficial flags and municipal flag renditions are hidden by default, including inherited provincial banners. A collapsed Sources & coverage disclosure in the sidebar footer contains an unchecked Include unofficial flags & renditions option; it appears only where optional artwork exists. Enabling it changes only flag images/captions and keeps their status labels. It does not change selection, map camera, statistics or URLs. The option resets on country departure and reload. Official municipal logo flags documented by a municipal protocol remain visible. The same policy applies to future flags marked optional or carrying unofficial/unverified/rendition metadata.
