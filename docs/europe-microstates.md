Monaco opens under France as a navigation subpage, following the existing Vatican City/Italy pattern. It remains identified as an independent sovereign state, uses Monaco’s own theme and national flag, and has a Back to France link. It is excluded from France’s regional quiz and from the top-level country menu. Both the French settings link and the map silhouette open Monaco directly.

Luxembourg uses the official ACT administrative-boundary download updated 5 October 2026 (CC0). Its 12 canton fills are dissolved from the same 100 commune polygons used for selection. Historical districts supplied in the download are excluded because they were abolished in 2015. Catalogue IDs retain the source LAU identifiers. The national display edge is aligned to the existing French, German and Belgian outlines inside a 300 m candidate corridor, eliminating survey gaps and overlaps. ACT’s original geometry remains retained unchanged. Cantons and communes are administrative areas, so separate invented city dots are not added.

San Marino has all nine castelli from geoBoundaries’ pinned OpenStreetMap/Wambacher extract, representing 2017. The external boundary is reconciled to the existing Italian enclave ring; internal castello divisions remain from that source. Small uncovered edge fragments are assigned to the nearest castello. Italy owns the complete shared international stroke, preventing duplicate outlines. No newer survey date is claimed.

Monaco’s national relation includes territorial waters. The displayed land is clipped to the OSM coastline retrieved on 19 October 2026, retaining Mareterra, port basins and offshore physical land. A 100 m candidate corridor is clipped to physical land and erased by the existing French outline, aligning the shared border without covering France. The resulting display area is approximately 2.0803 km². France’s local context is patched from its own detailed regional geometry so the inactive context follows the same edge. The national relation, filtered coastline ways, source URLs, query bounds and checksums are retained under scripts/additional-sources/europe-microstates/.

Statistics are rebuilt offline by scripts/prepare-europe-microstate-statistics.mjs from retained official downloads and their SHA-256 manifest under scripts/statistics-sources/europe-microstates/.
