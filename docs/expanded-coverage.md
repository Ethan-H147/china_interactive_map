# UK places, Indian districts and Turkish administrative levels

The UK settlement layer contains 33,590 points. It retains the 4,226 existing census-area records, including the combined London overview, and adds 29,364 named places. All 32,398 OS Open Names cities, towns, villages, hamlets and other settlements in Great Britain are represented, together with 336 OSNI towns/villages and 652 GeoNames populated places in Northern Ireland. Matching names within the same local authority and three kilometres share a record, as does a GeoNames entry that already locates a NISRA settlement (Castlereagh for Metropolitan Castlereagh). Separately named towns inside multi-town census areas remain separate points. All 33,386 source entries have an auditable display mapping; 164 entries within Greater London use its single London marker.

There is no population cutoff for the gazetteer additions. Existing census populations still describe their original whole statistical areas. Named places below that 1,000-resident base take official counts from `small-place-census.json.gz`: 3,452 match a smaller ONS 2022 built-up area (Census 2021), NRS 2022 settlement or NISRA settlement (Census 2021) of the same name within three kilometres, a joint settlement such as "Storth and Sandside" going to its nearest named component; 3,184 more match a namesake English parish or Welsh community within five kilometres that contains no built-up output area, so its Census 2021 count is the village with its outlying hamlets and farms. Altogether 10,862 of the 33,590 dots carry a census count. The other 22,728 (12,258 hamlets, 2,640 other settlements, 7,125 villages, 521 Northern Irish places and 184 OS towns or cities outside a matching census area) have no official count: they are below the NRS and NISRA settlement thresholds (about 500 residents) and outside any ONS built-up area or namesake rural parish. They receive no inferred population. Twenty-two small census settlements have no gazetteer place of their name nearby (mostly Northern Irish villages absent from OSNI and GeoNames) and are listed in the mapping audit.

Below about 2,900 residents the shared population zoom scale stops at 9.5, so UK records continue the same formula to closer zooms (a village of 350 appears near zoom 11.7, one of 100 near 13). Places without a count use the zoom of 500 residents (villages) or 100 residents (hamlets and other settlements). OS suburban-area records, historical/deserted GeoNames places and individual properties are excluded. This is comprehensive coverage of those published settlement classes, rather than a claim that every locally used place name exists in a national gazetteer. OSNI's published gazetteer is a coarse map-label collection, supplemented with GeoNames; some GeoNames coordinates are approximate or coincident. Coincident places remain searchable without stacking their dots. Dot density follows the existing settlement renderer.

OS Open Names is the October 2026 CSV release, verified against the download API's MD5. Bilingual English/Welsh and English/Gaelic names remain searchable. OSGB36 coordinates use the standard seven-parameter Helmert conversion to WGS84. Administrative parents come from polygon membership; ten coastal points just outside the simplified land outline use the nearest boundary, all within five kilometres, and are listed in the mapping audit. Source credits, licences and retained-file hashes are in `scripts/boundary-sources/uk-places/manifest.json`.

Run `npm run build:uk-places` to rebuild offline from the retained gazetteer extracts and statistical base. The existing `prepare-uk-settlements.mjs` also refreshes that base and reapplies the gazetteer expansion. `extract-uk-open-names.mjs path/to/opname_csv_gb.zip` reproduces the OS extract from its pinned archive without unpacking the entire postcode/road collection. NI source extracts are retained beside it. `npm run fetch:uk-small-places` rebuilds the small-place census file from the retained statistics extracts and ONS's output-area lookups and Census 2021 counts (cached under `artifacts/europe-source/uk-small-places`; pass `--refresh` to download again) and updates its manifest hash. The runtime loads compressed `settlements.bin`; JSON remains available for inspection.

India now has 785 district records under its 36 states and union territories, from the **LGD 2024** geometry snapshot republished by bharatlas. This is a dated snapshot: reorganisations after it are not represented. LGD identifiers are retained, except for the source's two uncoded claimed-territory features, Mirpur and Muzaffarabad, which use stable name-based identifiers. English source labels are retained without guessed translations; all existing state language profiles survive.

District shapes load by state or union territory. They are clipped to the existing state land masks, leaving state and international geometry unchanged. Smaller source districts take precedence over overlapping older source polygons; this repairs overlaps such as Sanchor inside the older Jalore polygon. No district is omitted. District area is mapped area for this snapshot; no district population is invented. Run `npm run build:india-districts` to rebuild from the retained, hashed source. The source manifest and per-state coverage audit are in `scripts/boundary-sources/india-districts/`.

Ladakh’s district survey left strips uncovered inside the retained state mask, exposing the parent highlight when selected. Its two district fills now cover that mask completely. Each missing strip joins the district sharing the longest edge; isolated mask islands join the nearest district. The state outline and international claims remain unchanged. The India builder reapplies this repair; `scripts/repair-ladakh-district-coverage.mjs` updates the existing Ladakh chunk alone. `npm run test:ladakh` checks coverage, overlaps, both sides of every outer-edge segment and mapped-area metadata. The `ladakh-selection` browser scenarios verify rendered colors on desktop/mobile in flat and globe modes.

Turkey retains its scoped loading: regions load first, provinces load for the selected region, and districts load for the selected province. The nationwide third-level bundle has been rolled back; existing names, municipal metadata and district search are unchanged.

`npm run test:expanded-coverage` checks complete source accounting, unchanged census figures and London behavior, district identities and hierarchy, preserved state geometry, language profiles, scoped Turkish loading, and desktop/mobile search, rendering and saved selections.
