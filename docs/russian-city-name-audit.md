Broader district identities (including Magadan’s districts, Гаринский, Сосьвинский, Алапаевское and Махнёвское) are retained rather than renamed after a different administrative centre. This is a name/presentation audit, not a boundary or present-day legal-status revision.

Verification now checks each retained census city name independently of the display formatter and generation plan, plus explicit regression fixtures for Владивосток, Ангарск, Петрозаводск, Арамиль and Мамоново. Valid adjective-shaped town names are protected from suffix stripping.
