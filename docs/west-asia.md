

## Saudi Northern Borders alignment correction

The national-mask fit had assigned a connected frontier gap to Hafar Al Batin in Eastern Province, producing a narrow westward needle along the Iraq border. The correction splits that added band at the retained Rafha–Hafar boundary endpoint and connects it to the nearest point of the existing Iraq frontier (about 388 metres). Approximately 48.155 km² of the added strip is assigned to Rafha, Northern Borders. Original governorate land, all other region geometries, national land, international strokes, the Khor Al Adaid correction and census populations remain unchanged. Region and governorate geometry, shared internal lines, label points, bounds, area cards and population-density comparisons are regenerated together.

The correction runs during the Saudi depth build. `npm run build:saudi-northern-border` applies it to an existing checkout; `npm run test:saudi-northern-border` checks seven former needle locations, the complete national outline and desktop/phone selection of Northern Borders and Rafha. The shared West Asia depth and Khor Al Adaid checks also cover hierarchy and the preserved coast.
