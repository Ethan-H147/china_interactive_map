The world background uses the pinned Natural Earth 5.1.2 1:10m country polygons retained under scripts/boundary-sources/nordics/. All 258 source countries, dependencies and separately coded areas are accounted for: countries already covered by atlas pages keep their existing shapes and frontier ownership; the remaining countries have light-gray land and noninteractive outlines. Adding these outlines does not register new selectable countries or change the country log.

scripts/build-world-land.mjs builds the background polygons and scripts/world-country-topology.mjs extracts their shared arcs. Neighboring background countries share one edge. The country polygons are simplified together with a 100 m threshold and keep-shapes before overlays; islands and small states are retained. Atlas portal geometry is a fixed reference. Foreign overlap is clipped, and uncovered old-reference land within an 8 km land-frontier corridor is assigned to the nearest foreign edge, excluding source lake polygons. This reconciles display geometry; the 1:10m dataset remains a generalized reference rather than a surveyed boundary or a change in territorial claims. Existing disputed-border and control-line handling stays in the country adapters. The separately coded Baykonur Cosmodrome, Brazilian Island and Southern Patagonian Ice Field are already inside existing atlas silhouettes; they do not acquire duplicate country outlines.

Coasts of background countries are outlined too. Edges already owned by atlas frontiers are omitted segment by segment. A final segment-ownership pass removes microscopic floating-point retraces instead of fading away portions of a border. The line layer stays above fills, remains visible through zoom, and participates in the existing satellite scene rules. It is absent from country click routing. Ready portals and the active country's detailed map immediately hide their temporary background copies.

French and British overseas areas, Crown Dependencies, and New Zealand's associated territories use the silhouettes already present in their atlas pages. The temporary atlas fallback is simplified in angular coordinates because some territories use unwrapped longitudes across the date line. Full country detail continues to load from the existing country-specific sources. The new compact world land and outline files are preloaded in parallel; budget assertions bound decoded data and compressed downloads.

Vietnam's obsolete geoBoundaries outline was still present in china-neighbours.bin under the detailed Quang Ninh coast. That geometry is removed from both the retained runtime output and future China-neighbor builds. A visible detailed portal also suppresses older copies in the neighbor band. The regression test samples six formerly filled offshore points near Ha Long / Bai Tu Long; none can have a rendered land fill, on desktop or mobile, or after switching countries.

Rebuild with npm run build:world-land (which also refreshes compact copies). Validate with npm run test:world-land, npm run test:world-outlines, npm run test:compact and node scripts/browser/run.mjs runtime. The outline validator checks source coverage, unique segment ownership, geometry/fill alignment, fixed data budgets and Vietnam's obsolete source exclusion. Real-browser checks sample actual rendered pixels along six international borders and verify that clicking a background country does not switch atlas pages.
