# Netherlands football snapshot

Reviewed 29 October 2026 for the 2026–27 season. Eredivisie has 18 clubs, Eerste Divisie 20, and Tweede Divisie 18: cumulative quizzes contain 18 / 38 / 56 clubs.

The retained official Eredivisie and Keuken Kampioen Divisie team pages establish the top two rosters. KNVB’s 2026–27 allocation PDF establishes all 18 third-tier members. ESPN provides structured top-two rosters and authentic crest artwork; the current-season Dutch Wikipedia table supplies the third-tier names and venue references, reviewed against KNVB. Its extracted rows are labeled as reviewed extraction in the manifest. The older English general Tweede Divisie page is retained only for audit, not used as the season roster.

Grounds use named Wikidata stadium coordinates, named OpenStreetMap football facilities, or Europlan’s named pitch coordinates. Review records preserve the explicit choice. Stadium references identify an actual ground or football facility, rather than a city centre. Rohda and Hoek use the club-ground address point within their football facility. Jong AZ is in Wijdewormer, not the provider’s stale Den Haag location. Helmond Sport uses its new MySteel Stadion. Jong Sparta uses Robey Academy, not Het Kasteel. Jong Almere City normally plays on Academyveld/field 4, not the main Yanmar stadium; the primary August fixture announcement documents its exceptional move into the senior stadium. RKAV uses KWABO, separately from FC Volendam’s Kras Stadion. IJsselmeervogels and Spakenburg have separate adjacent pitches within De Westmaat and must not be grouped.

`third-review.json` holds reviewed names, aliases and original crest URLs. Original club crests are retained under `crests/`, with uncompressed artwork snapshots and provider replies hashed in `manifest.json`. Images are authentic club badges, including the parent badge for reserve teams. Reserve aliases exclude the senior team’s name. Copyright and trademarks remain with their owners. OpenStreetMap geometry is © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL); Wikidata coordinates are CC0. Wikimedia artwork retains its individual source-page licensing.

`node scripts/football-sources/netherlands/build.mjs` recreates `clubs.json` offline from retained provider responses and review files. `node scripts/football-sources/netherlands/validate.mjs` checks all raw and normalized hashes, images, counts, sources, aliases and the distinct reserve/adjacent grounds. Source updates require deliberate review; existing snapshots are checksum-verified and never silently refetched. Rebuild runtime assets with `node scripts/build-football-quizzes.mjs netherlands`.
