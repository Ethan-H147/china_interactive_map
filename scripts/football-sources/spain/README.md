# Spain football quiz source snapshot

2026–27 men's club membership is pinned to LaLiga's official 20-club and 22-club directories and the RFEF's approved two 20-club Primera Federación groups. Cumulative difficulties contain 20, 42, and 82 clubs. Reserve sides are distinct clubs. FC Andorra remains at its real ground in Encamp; Ceuta and both Canary Island clubs are retained.

`clubs.json` is the reviewed normalized input. Each row has a stable club ID, league tier, brief name, aliases, actual ground, locality, longitude/latitude, coordinate source and crest source. `crests/` contains PNG versions fitting inside 256×256 while preserving the authentic crest proportions. `original-crests/` retains the fetched artwork compressed; `snapshots/` retains the source roster pages, geographic responses and selected official venue confirmations. `manifest.json` pins uncompressed SHA-256 and byte counts for compressed snapshots/originals, and direct SHA-256 for PNGs.

The top two divisions use LaLiga's current official venue names, rather than historical club home-ground claims. Explicit checks include Betis at La Cartuja, Andorra at Encamp, Zaragoza at temporary Ibercaja Stadium, Celta Fortuna at Balaídos, Atlético Madrileño at Alcalá de Henares, and CE Europa at the natural-grass Can Dragó athletics infield. Real Sociedad B is placed on the actual Z7 pitch authorized in January 2026, rather than the old José Luis Orbegozo field. Villarreal B uses the Mini Estadi, confirmed in a September 2026 official match report; the English Wikipedia season table incorrectly names La Cerámica. The true Celta/Celta Fortuna shared ground is kept as a shared coordinate. Nearby reserve grounds are not artificially offset.

Coordinates are sourced from retained Wikidata geographic claims (CC0), OpenStreetMap pitch geometries (ODbL, © OpenStreetMap contributors), or the Antequera municipal sports department's own location link. Bibliotecario/Infopoint geocodes were not used: one suggested Coria point was in the wrong part of town. The named OSM Coria pitch and primary ground/address references resolve it.

Crests are official LaLiga directory artwork for tiers 1 and 2, and club-identification artwork served by Wikipedia for tier 3. Atlético Madrileño and Real Madrid Castilla use their parent club's crest. Crest copyrights and trademarks remain with the respective clubs; no blanket open-image licence is asserted. PNG conversion does not change the artwork. The source files and URLs remain inspectable in the manifest.

Checked locally: official roster counts; 20+20 third-tier groups; unique stable IDs; all 82 actual-ground coordinates; all 82 valid crest PNGs; all retained source hashes and image dimensions. A contact sheet in ignored artifacts was visually inspected.
