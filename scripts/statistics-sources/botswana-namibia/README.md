Population inputs use the final Botswana December 2022 census locality report and Namibia's 2023 census constituency tables. Each compact JSON file preserves the official source URL and retrieval date.

`download-namibia.mjs` extracts constituency population and area from the Namibia Statistics Agency's census portal. The portal's Oniipa population has a missing digit and its Onayena area is malformed; the extractor uses 33,727 and 442.34 km² from the final Oshikoto regional report (September 2025), Table 2.2. Constituency populations then sum exactly to the published national total, 3,022,401. City populations are prepared separately from Appendix III of the final national census report; the older portal town tables are not used.

`extract-botswana.mjs` reads a local text extraction of the official December 2022 report at the URL in `botswana-census-2022.json`. Download that PDF as `botswana-census-localities-2022.pdf` and extract its text with `pypdf` to `botswana-census-localities-2022.txt` before rerunning. The 28 census district counts sum exactly to 2,359,609. Ten geographical district totals combine their census districts, including separately administered enclosed cities and towns. The builder validates that every displayed child and parent has a matched record and that child populations sum to the parent.

Areas for Namibian constituencies are published census areas. Region areas are their sums. Botswana area cards use the displayed boundary polygons and are explicitly marked as mapped estimates. No verified official district or regional GDP series was found for either country, so the builder does not allocate national GDP to subdivisions.
